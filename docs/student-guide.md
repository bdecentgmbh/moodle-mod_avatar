# Avatar — Student guide

In an avatar activity you collect avatars and upgrade them.

## Picking an avatar

Open the avatar activity, or find its avatars on the course page. Click **Pick** below an avatar to
add it to your collection. It becomes your primary avatar.

Click an avatar to see its page: its description, its tags and its variants.

## Upgrading an avatar

An avatar can have several variants. Click **Upgrade** to get the next one. Once you have collected
an avatar, its page shows its secret information.

Your teacher's settings may limit how many avatars you can collect, or when. The activity then shows
a message.

## My avatars

*My avatars* in the user menu lists all your avatars, wherever you collected them. There you can
choose your **primary avatar** with *Set as primary*. If your site uses avatars as profile
pictures, your primary avatar becomes your profile picture.
