# Avatar — Teacher guide

In an avatar activity, participants pick avatars from the site's avatar library and upgrade them
to their next variants. Their collection appears on their *My avatars* page, and their primary
avatar can become their profile picture.

## Adding an avatar activity

1. Turn editing on in your course.
2. Choose *Add an activity or resource* and pick **Avatar**.
3. Enter a **Name** and, optionally, a **Description**.
4. Under **Appearance**:
   - **Avatar selection**: *All avatars* offers every active avatar of the library; *Specific tags*
     offers only those with one of the tags you enter in **Specific tags** (comma separated).
   - **Display mode**: show the avatars on the activity page, or directly on the course page.
   - **Header content** and **Footer content**: shown above and below the avatars.
   - **Empty state**: shown when the activity offers no avatars.
5. Save.

Addons may add more settings, for example collection limits.

## Completion

Besides viewing the activity, completion can require that the participant **collects an avatar**
in the activity. An avatar stays linked to the activity in which it was collected, even when it is
upgraded elsewhere.

## What participants see

Each avatar shows its image, its progress through the variants and a **Pick** or **Upgrade**
button. Clicking an avatar opens its page with the description, the tags, the variants and, once
collected, the secret information.

## Backup, restore and duplicate

Duplicating the activity or importing it into another course on the same site keeps its avatars.
When you restore an activity from another site, avatars that do not exist on this site can only be
created by users who may manage the avatar library; ask your manager if avatars are missing.
